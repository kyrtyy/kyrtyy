Research & Technical Pillars

Pillar 01

Stochastic Systems & Statistical Physics

Non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, Langevin dynamics, Fokker-Planck evolution, and the thermodynamics of learning. Viewing gradient descent as relaxation toward thermal equilibrium.

Modern Machine Learning & AI

Generative modeling via stochastic differential equations (score-based diffusion), physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), joint embeddings, and high-dimensional representation dynamics.

Quantum Theory & Field Systems

Quantum mechanics, Feynman path integrals, and formal quantum computing foundations. Exploring quantum states and density operator evolution in noisy quantum information systems.
