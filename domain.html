Research & Thematic Pillars

A systematic overview of my foundational investigations spanning non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, generative learning dynamics, quantum technology, and high-performance numerical physics.

01

Stochastic Systems & Non-Equilibrium Statistical Physics

Classical mechanics studies trajectories determined by exact equations of motion. However, complex systems—from microscopic Brownian particles to neural networks undergoing mini-batch SGD—are driven by fluctuations. My work approaches these systems through non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, where entropy production and stochastic drift govern macroscopic phenomena.

$$\mathrm{d}X_t = -\nabla V(X_t)\,\mathrm{d}t + \sqrt{2\beta^{-1}}\,\mathrm{d}W_t \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \partial_t p(x,t) = \nabla \cdot \left(p(x,t)\nabla V(x) + \beta^{-1}\nabla p(x,t)\right)$$

Langevin Dynamics & Itô Calculus

Formulating continuous-time Markovian processes with stochastic differentials. Exploring the transition between underdamped (inertial) and overdamped regimes, and the convergence of numerical integrators (Euler-Maruyama, BAOAB).

Thermodynamics of Learning

Treating gradient descent as an open thermodynamic system relaxing in an energetic loss landscape. Analyzing how stochastic noise from mini-batches acts as an effective temperature parameter facilitating barrier-crossing.

Fokker-Planck Evolution

Tracking probability distribution densities under drift and diffusion fields. Investigating stationary states, Gibbs-Boltzmann measures, and non-equilibrium steady states (NESS) with non-zero probability currents.

Dynamical Game Theory

Analyzing multi-agent interactions and strategic equilibria as coupled stochastic differential systems. Understanding how local utility optimization gives rise to emergent macroscopic organization.

02

Modern Machine Learning & AI: Physics-Informed Dynamics

Standard deep learning treats neural networks as unconstrained function approximators, requiring massive datasets to avoid pathological failure modes. My focus is on embedding physical symmetries, differential operators, and continuous-time dynamics directly into the model architecture.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{PINN}}(\theta) = \frac{1}{N_d}\sum_{i=1}^{N_d} |u_\theta(x_i, t_i) - u_i|^2 + \frac{\lambda}{N_f}\sum_{j=1}^{N_f} \left|\mathcal{N}_x[u_\theta](x_j, t_j) - f(x_j, t_j)\right|^2$$

Continuous-Time Diffusion Models

Score-based generative models framed as forward-backward SDEs. Perturbing complex data distributions to Gaussian noise via Ornstein-Uhlenbeck drift, then reversing time using neural score estimators \(\nabla_x \log p_t(x)\).

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

Solving forward and inverse partial differential equations by encoding PDE residuals directly into backpropagation graphs. Utilizing automatic differentiation to compute exact derivatives without mesh discretization.

Operator Learning (FNO & DeepONet)

Learning infinite-dimensional mappings between function spaces rather than discrete vectors. Fourier Neural Operators (FNO) parameterize kernel integrals in frequency space, allowing zero-shot resolution transfer.

Representation Learning & Symmetries

Joint embedding architectures and equivariant networks respecting physical conservation laws (momentum, mass, energy) and geometric Lie groups (\(SO(3)\), \(SE(3)\)).

03

Quantum Theory & Quantum Technology

As part of my academic minor in Quantum Technology at IISc Bengaluru, I study the foundations of quantum mechanics, path integrals, and open quantum systems. Understanding coherent superposition, entanglement, and decoherence provides deep insights into information limits and non-classical dynamics.

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}\rho}{\mathrm{d}t} = -\frac{i}{\hbar}[\hat{H}, \rho] + \sum_k \left( \hat{L}_k \rho \hat{L}_k^\dagger - \frac{1}{2}\{\hat{L}_k^\dagger \hat{L}_k, \rho\} \right) \quad\text{[Lindblad Master Equation]}$$

Feynman Path Integrals & QFT

Connecting quantum transitions to functional integrals over all possible field configurations. Wick rotation (\(t \to -i\tau\)) mapping quantum field theory directly onto Euclidean statistical mechanics partition functions.

Open Quantum Systems

Describing system-bath interactions where environmental coupling induces decoherence and dissipation. Solving master equations for quantum state fidelity in realistic, noisy quantum hardware.

Quantum Computing Fundamentals

Quantum circuits, unitary transformations, quantum Fourier transform (QFT), variational quantum eigensolvers (VQE), and error mitigation strategies in NISQ-era devices.

Quantum State Tomography

Reconstructing unknown quantum density operators \(\rho\) from projective measurement statistics, evaluating purity, von Neumann entropy, and entanglement measures.

04

Computational Physics & High-Performance Systems

Theoretical formulas require computational scale to tackle realistic physical geometries and multiscale turbulence. I build scalable numerical pipelines utilizing GPU acceleration, parallel computing, and spectral methods.

$$\text{Speedup } S(P) = \frac{1}{(1-f) + \frac{f}{P}} \quad\text{combined with memory bandwidth saturation analysis on modern CUDA streaming multiprocessors}$$

Numerical Fluid Dynamics (CFD)

Direct numerical simulation (DNS) and finite-difference / finite-volume schemes for the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations. Solving pressure Poisson equations and resolving boundary layer gradients.

GPU Computing & CUDA

Writing high-throughput parallel kernels, coalesced global memory access patterns, shared memory cache utilization, and multi-stream concurrent execution on NVIDIA hardware.

Spectral & Pseudo-Spectral Methods

Transforming continuous spatial derivatives into algebraic multiplications via Fast Fourier Transforms (\(\partial_x \to ik_x\)), achieving exponential convergence for smooth periodic flows.

Automatic Differentiation vs Classical Solvers

Benchmarking the memory overhead of autograd backward calculation graphs against lean, memory-efficient classical finite-difference stencils in large-scale simulation grids.
