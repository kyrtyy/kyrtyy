Analog Dimensions & Pursuits

Physics and computation are rigorous, but they are incomplete without tactile physical activities, acoustic resonance, and visual narratives. Here is where the laboratory gives way to the open field, wooden fretboards, and 35mm film stock.

01

Ultimate Frisbee: Kinetics & Field Awareness

Ultimate is defined by rapid non-inertial acceleration, continuous multi-agent spatial coordination, and aerodynamic flight physics. Unlike a ball that follows a simple ballistic parabola, a 175-gram disc generates aerodynamic lift as its gyroscopic spin stabilizes Bernoulli pressure differentials across the airfoil.

On the field, I look for cutting lanes and timing: reading wind velocity shear, throwing outside-in (OI) and inside-out (IO) curves, and anticipating defensive rotations. There is a rare, quiet flow state during a high-speed point where decisions happen without conscious deliberation—a distributed physical system executing emergent strategies without a central supervisor.

Crucially, Ultimate is self-officiated even at world-championship levels under the Spirit of the Game. It demands mutual respect, rigorous honesty under physical exhaustion, and direct player accountability—a rare social contract in competitive sport.

"A disc in flight is a dynamic system balancing lift, drag, and gyroscopic precession. On the field, you learn to throw not to where a teammate is, but to where the aerodynamic trajectory and their velocity vector intersect in space." // On Field Kinetics & Anticipation
02

Acoustic & Electric Guitar: Resonance without Software

Spending days in front of Linux terminals and GPU computation graphs makes tactile mechanical sound essential. The guitar is pure classical mechanics: tensioned steel or phosphor-bronze strings oscillating over fixed boundary conditions:

$$f_n = \frac{n}{2L} \sqrt{\frac{T}{\mu}}, \qquad n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$$

I explore acoustic fingerstyle, open tunings (DADGAD, Open D), and modal chord voicings. Plucking a harmonic node at \(L/2\) or \(L/3\) isolates pure standing-wave modes. The vibration couples through the bone saddle to the spruce soundboard, projecting acoustic resonance through an air cavity governed by Helmholtz resonance.

With electric guitars, electromagnetic pickups translate physical string motion into fluctuating magnetic flux, producing subtle tube saturation and harmonic distortion. It is an immediate reminder that beautiful phenomena occur through physical continuous coupling without a single line of software.

03

World Cinema & Visual Storytelling

I have a deep devotion to auteur world cinema—films that treat cinema not as rapid commercial entertainment, but as an art of time, composition, and interior reflection.

I am drawn to directors who understand temporal rhythm and architectural framing:

  • Andrei Tarkovsky: "Sculpting in Time." His long, meditative tracking shots (in Stalker, Mirror, and Solaris) allow the audience to experience time itself as a physical substance.
  • Stanley Kubrick: Uncompromising geometric perspective, one-point symmetry, and calculated pacing that views human fallibility from an almost cosmic distance.
  • Abbas Kiarostami: Blurring the boundary between documentary realism and fiction, finding profound human truth in patient car conversations traversing rural Iranian landscapes.
  • Edward Yang: Capturing modern urban alienation, memory, and generational transition through intricate multi-threaded architectural compositions (in Yi Yi and A Brighter Summer Day).

Cinema, like theoretical physics, is about observing reality with uncompromising patience and discovering the hidden architecture that connects fleeting moments.
