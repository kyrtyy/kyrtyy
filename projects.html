Robots

2026

Latent world models for laggy teleoperation

A 5M-parameter transformer on frozen DINOv2 features predicts a pushing task 600 ms ahead, so the operator sees where things will be, not where they were. 66% lower position error (80% lower on orientation) than the best classical predictor. A predictive "ghost" display recovers 86–90% of the performance lost to 300 ms of jittery delay. A chunked executor cut robot freezes from 161 to 2 per minute.

Simulated, with a scripted operator. Every claim was tested against a gate written down before the data existed, and one hypothesis came back null, which I report.

2026

FlowPolicy

A few-step flow-matching robot policy, written as a LeRobot plugin. One flow pass plus three diffusion refinement steps reaches 66.3% success on PushT in ~19 ms; 100-step Diffusion Policy gets 67.8% in 383 ms. That's ~20× faster for 1.5 points. Along the way I fixed two baselines that were quietly handicapped, so the comparison is fair.

Markets

2026

MT-PINN market maker

A physics-informed network that solves the Avellaneda–Stoikov HJB equation, exported to a C++20 / LibTorch engine fed by a lock-free ring buffer. On an out-of-sample BTCUSDT window it gets +19% Sharpe and 37% lower max drawdown than the closed-form strategy, for the same PnL.

2026

Auditing TradingAgents

I reimplemented a multi-agent LLM trading paper (arXiv 2412.20138) on self-hosted Qwen3-30B and Llama-3.1-8B via vLLM: ~21k decisions, ~0.4B tokens. The paper reports a 26.6% three-month return as 30.5% annualized; it is actually ~165%. And on the same window, a random strategy scored a Sharpe of 3.58.

2026

Point-in-time market data

A price pipeline for crypto and equities (Yahoo, Binance, Kraken → TimescaleDB) and a point-in-time fundamentals set built from SEC XBRL filings, so backtests can't see the future. It caught a 1000× share-count reporting slip in NVIDIA's filings.
